Rules for armenian-training-0.1. This application's Armenian Blot variant; do not substitute other Belote variants. English rule text, but answer the player in Russian. J=jack, Q=queen, K=king, A=ace.
Players: SOUTH/WEST/NORTH/EAST clockwise; SOUTH+NORTH vs WEST+EAST. 32 distinct cards, four suits, ranks 7,8,9,10,J,Q,K,A; 8 cards each. Random initial dealer; next dealer clockwise after each deal/redeal. Shuffle each deal, deal two rounds of four starting at dealer.next(). Dealer.next() bids/leads first; trick winner leads next. Four opening passes with no bid: no score, redeal with next dealer (R25). Target 301 small points, scores update only after the complete deal. If both teams reach target together, contracting team wins if contract succeeds, otherwise defenders win. Reverse caput is not automatic match loss (R31).
Bidding (R24,R26,R27): integer n>=8, any higher opening allowed, next bid strictly higher by >=1 regardless of suit. Four suits and no-trump (boy; trump=null) have equal bidding rank. May raise partner/change suit/no-trump. Passing does not withdraw from later bidding. A bid resets consecutive passes; FOUR consecutive passes end bidding, so after three passes bidder still acts. No artificial maximum bid. Contracting team=last bidder's team. Optional promised caput requires n>=26 and ALL 8 tricks; normal bids above 25 remain allowed. Once promised, later bids must also promise caput; suit can change.
Double/redouble (R06,R15,R20,R28,R29,R33): while bidding remains open either defender may double out of turn, regardless of previous passes; not after four closing passes. Double ends numeric bidding and freezes contract. Each contracting player separately may redouble or decline. One decline does not remove partner's right; both decline => doubled play; either redoubles => redoubled play. Multiplier m=1/2/4 applies ONLY to bid n, never card points, caput base or bonuses; same in trump/no-trump.
Ranks descending: trump J,9,A,10,K,Q,8,7; other suits and no-trump A,10,K,Q,J,9,8,7. Card points (R19), rank order A,10,K,Q,J,9,8,7: trump [11,10,4,3,20,14,0,0]; non-trump [11,10,4,3,2,0,0,0]; no-trump [19,10,4,3,2,0,0,0]. Card total 152 + last trick 10 =162. Ranking is not point value.
Legal play: leader any card. Follow led non-trump suit if held, no obligation to raise. Trump led: follow trump and overtrump highest trump on table if possible EVEN if partner winning, otherwise any trump (R02); if no trumps any card. Non-trump led, void, enemy winning: must play a trump that wins if any; if cannot overtrump, ANY card including non-trump allowed (R01). Void, partner winning: may discard non-trump; if voluntarily trumping must overtrump highest table trump if possible (R03). No-trump: follow led suit if held, else any card; never obligation to raise (R19). Winner highest trump, otherwise highest card of led suit; in no-trump only led suit can win. Off-suit non-trump cannot win by rank alone.
Declarations: runs use ascending 7,8,9,10,J,Q,K,A of ONE suit, within ONE hand; no combining partners' cards. Run length3=20,4=50,5..7=100; all8 can split disjoint 3+5 for120 (R08,R13). Four-of-kind points trump/no-trump: J200/200,9=140/100,A110/190,10/K/Q100/100,8=0/0 special cancellation,7=no declaration. Blot-reblot trump K+Q same hand=20; none in no-trump. Ordinary declarations cannot share a card; blot may overlap them (R12). Engine selects compatible set: maximize strongest single declaration priority, then total points, deterministic tie by suits SPADES,HEARTS,DIAMONDS,CLUBS then ascending ranks.
Comparison (R09,R10): compare team's strongest SINGLE declaration, not sum. Winner counts all its compatible timely declared/shown combinations; loser none. Blot never loses comparison. Trump: points first; equal points four-of-kind beats run; four-of-kind priority J>9>A>10>K>Q; equal-value runs higher top rank wins, then trump suit, then earlier seat from dealer.next(). No-trump PROVISIONAL R21: four-of-kind order J>9>A>10>K>Q REGARDLESS of value (four9=100 beats fourA=190). Runs compare points, top rank, then seat; no trump preference; equal points four-of-kind beats run. R23: four8 gives 0, cancels ALL enemy ordinary declarations, keeps own eligible declarations; does not cancel blot. Both teams cannot hold four8.
Timing (R11): strongest ordinary declaration before own first-trick card; eligible combinations shown before own second-trick card (already played proof cards are not returned to hand). Blot at first played trump K/Q; reblot at second, either order. Late declaration/show => no bonus; losing ordinary declarations never restored by score distribution. In profile0.1 ordinary announcement/show and blot are automatic, visible only at prescribed times: comparison attributes first, eligible proof cards second; do not expose other hidden hands.
Contract success (R07,R30,R32,R34): before rounding, own won card points incl last10 + own COUNTED bonuses >=10*n. Need >=1 team trick even if bonuses suffice (R34); need not outscore defenders. Actual caput=all8 tricks by one team, whether promised or not. For checking a contracting team's caput use250 INSTEAD OF162 card points, then own bonuses, no extra last10. Promised caput additionally requires all8. All8 may still fail a high bid if250+own bonuses<10*n. Failed promised caput with7 tricks fails even if points suffice. Promise alone does not set caput base.
Scoring (R14,R16,R17,R22,R30-R33): small points. Ordinary deal: defenderSmall=floor((defenderCardPoints+5)/10), contractSmall=16-defenderSmall; exclude declaration bonuses from rounding. Examples card117:45=>small11:5;106:56=>10:6;147:15=>14:2. Actual caput base25 to team taking all8, no ordinary rounding. Let C,D=counted own bonuses/10 (contractors/defenders), n=bid, m=multiplier, B=25 if EITHER team actually caputs else16. Success undoubled ordinary: [n+contractSmall+C, defenderSmall+D]. Success undoubled contracting caput: [n+25+C,D]. Success doubled/redoubled: [n*m+B+C+D,0]. ANY failure: [0,n*m+B+C+D]. Only counted bonuses transferred once, blot included; no resurrection of losing declarations. Reverse caput uses base25, not automatic match loss (R31).
